# Seele dogfight prototype

Source: owner-provided `assets/reference/seele/dragon_dogfight`, inspected October 30, 2026.
The original dragon/drone models and environment textures are retained unmodified for the local experiment and rollback.

The additional Space Dragon Spacecraft mechanical candidate is a local procedural armor/material revision over the retained source rig and flexible geometry. Its red game palette follows the owner's later direction; the blue Blender v002 source remains unchanged. Hidden recovery meshes, review studio and reference images are excluded from the runtime export. This revision is a local candidate, not final art or public-release approval.
