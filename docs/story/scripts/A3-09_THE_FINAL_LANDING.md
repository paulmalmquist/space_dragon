## A3-09 — Your Own Note

**[CINE]** The Rest Garden. Morrow's eyes are open, but he hasn't moved yet. The whole garden waits. Then, as the song washes back through Farport, each suit **hums**. It isn't the SOS. It's a single note, a different one for each of them.

> LYLA: "My suit's singing."
>
> CALLER: "That's *your* note. The scale's been listening to you the whole way."

**[CINE]** One by one, each suit **blooms**: the living material opens like petals, glows in its wearer's color, and settles back, changed. The scale gifts and the Farport upgrade fuse into **one power** each. Each reveal is under ten seconds. **[MUSIC]** Each family member's motif plays on their instrument, blends, and **morphs into its synth voice** (owner direction, SD-022): brass, cello, strings, marimba and celesta, one after another, each turning to synth, then all together. The motifs stay recognizable. These are sounds the old song never had, a **new song**, and Morrow can't hold what was never in his.

| Who | Power (draft) | Reveal moment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paul** | **Anchor:** hold anything still | A falling lantern stops in midair, then settles gently to the ground |
| **Maria** | **Sanctuary:** a dome where everyone heals and anything held is freed | The garden's held birds wake inside her dome and sing |
| **Luke** | **Lane Weaver:** lines of light that become rails and bridges | A glowing rail arcs up to the Roosts, and he rides it down |
| **Luna** | **Splashdown:** a wave of pink goo the family surfs | She surfs the dry fountain full of goo, shrieking with joy |

*Draft powers are in the [overview](../ACTS_II_III_THE_ROAD_TO_VIERA.md) § Suits to powers and stay placeholders. Guardrails: every power switches off on request, helmets always open, and the suit never controls its wearer.*

**[CINE]** The Lanemother breathes on the family's dragon. Its wings shimmer: **Lane Wings**, short blink-jumps. The Core, back on in the dragon's chest, chirps for the first time since Landfall.

> CORE: "Good morning."

**[PLAY]** **Five Powers.** Five Together's final form, tried once on a harmless target (a big overgrown hedge, which becomes a perfectly trimmed dragon topiary). The chord **resolves to the Heartnote**.

## A3-10 — The Great Launch and the Last Hold

**[CINE]** A rumble from the city. Then another. All across Farport, travelers who were held mid-goodbye finish their goodbyes and do what Vierans do: **they go.** Ships lift off the landing fields. The Mooring Ring unclamps. Within a minute the sky over Viera is **full of ships**, all leaving at once.

> LUKE: "Whoa. *Everybody's* leaving?"
>
> CALLER: "That's… just what we do."

In the garden, Morrow watches them go. Every ship. Every traveler. All at once. The thing he feared most, filling the whole sky.

**[CINE]** **Morrow stands up.** It's the first time he has moved in the entire game. It should feel like a mountain standing. Stones crack. The Lanemother is thrown back. His wings open and **blot out Viera's sun**. **[MUSIC]** The held note returns, enormous, bent and furious.

> MORROW (the only time he raises his voice): "**STAY.**"

> PAUL: "Everybody in. One more."

### Phase 1 — The sky

**[PLAY]** Flight, at the chosen tier. The dragon launches from the garden with Lane Wings and climbs into a sky full of fleeing ships, and Morrow rises after them, the largest thing in the game.
- **Holds:** he hurls gold bands across the sky, catching ships mid-launch and freezing them in place. **Song-breath** breaks each Hold, and every ship freed is one more that gets to leave. **[MUSIC]** Each freed ship adds a voice to the song.
- **Wingmen:** the four star-dragons dive in from the edge of the Quiet, which is gone now. The hatchlings follow, with the passenger riding in front. The re-tuned **Sweeper** tows freed ships clear of the fight. Every traveler ship that's free flies in formation behind the family.
- **Lane Wings** blink the dragon past his sweeping wings. **Formation calls:** "Break left!" "Climb!" Missing a call has no penalty.
- The **Heartnote Roar**, the dragon's final finisher, breaks every Hold on screen at once.
- **[MUSIC]** The completed answer with rests, every world's part woven in, the family's new synth voices leading over the dragon horn, all of it against his one note.

Phase progress is saved. A bonk rejoins at the start of the current wave.

### Phase 2 — His back

**[CINE]** Morrow turns to catch the whole fleet in one great Hold. The dragon blinks in close and **lands on his back**. The family jumps down onto tarnished gold scales the size of rooftops.

**[PLAY]** On foot, with the new powers, the family climbs toward his throat, where the stolen Heartnote burns like a trapped star.
- Paul's **Anchor** holds a beating wing still long enough to cross it.
- Luke's **Lane Weaver** rails the family across the gap between his shoulders.
- Luna's **Splashdown** surfs everyone up the long slope of his spine; Lyla's **Echo** copies it for a second wave.
- Maria's **Sanctuary** shelters the family as his gaze sweeps over them, and frees anyone a Hold catches.
- **[MUSIC]** The family's synth voices, one per power, each a phrase of the new song.

Phase progress is saved. A full-family bonk resumes at the start of this phase.

### Phase 3 — The choice

**[CINE]** His throat. Up close, the Heartnote is small, and bright, and caught in his jaws like something he can't put down. Morrow is still. He could shake them off. He doesn't. **[MUSIC]** Everything drops away except the held note and, very faintly, the Old Song.

Nobody strikes.

**[PLAY]** **Lyla's Holo Replay.** The prompt: "Lyla, play him his song." She plays the Old Song from her archive, the Caller's voice: *"…and she named him Morrow, because he was tomorrow."*

Morrow's eyes close for a moment.

> LUKE (stepping forward): "You could come with us."
>
> *(beat)*
>
> LUKE: "You could go somewhere too."
>
> MORROW: "If I let go, they leave."

**[CINE]** The Lanemother rises beside them, enormous and awake, and says his name for the first time since the first night.

> LANEMOTHER (softly): "Morrow."
>
> MORROW: "...Mother."
>
> LANEMOTHER: "They always leave. And they always come home. That is what home is *for*."
>
> MORROW: "...I only wanted them to come home."
>
> LANEMOTHER: "I know. Let go."

**[PLAY]** One prompt, and it waits as long as the player needs: **"Let him choose."** The player doesn't press anything to make him; holding still is the choice. *(After a few seconds of stillness, it happens. Pressing a button just makes the family move closer.)*

**[CINE]** He opens his jaws.

The **Heartnote** flies free, up and out, a small bright light that splits into every lane at once. Across the whole universe, every song that has been waiting finally lands on its last note. **[MUSIC]** The Great Song **resolves**. All of it. Everywhere.

But he has held for so long that he can no longer move. His wings don't close. His scales stiffen into light, from the tips inward. The family is lifted gently off his back by the dragon and set down in the air beside him.

> LYLA: "...He's turning into a star."

Slowly, Morrow rises off Viera into the sky. He doesn't fight it. He looks down once at the fleet of ships, all of them leaving, all of them free, and for the first time he doesn't look afraid. Then he is a single point of light, high above Farport, that **never moves**.

> CALLER: "He always wanted them to steer by him."
>
> LANEMOTHER: "Now they will."

**[MUSIC]** Morrow's drone resolves into one high, pure, sustained tone: **the same held note, finally beautiful**. It will play softly under every night scene on Viera from now on.

## A3-11 — The Final Landing

### A3-11a — Formation Home (the victory lap)

**[PLAY]** Flight. The dragon leads **the whole fleet** down to Farport under the new star: the four star-dragons, the Lanemother gliding at the head, the hatchlings, thousands of traveler ships, and the Sweeper at the back, towing anyone who needs a hand.
- **A ring run with no enemies**, through the Mooring Ring, between the Roosts, low over the Departure Halls.
- Each ring passed adds the family's voices, in key. Formation calls are light and fun, and missing one has no penalty.
- **[MUSIC]** The full song with rests and the Heartnote, synth and orchestra together. This is the fighter-jet system's victory lap.

Skipping the flight leads straight to A3-11b with the same state.

### A3-11b — Fair Lanes (the homecoming)

**[CINE]** The main landing field, packed with travelers, every one of them awake. **[MUSIC]** The Caller's theme, with a whole crowd singing it.

A pair of travelers pushes through the crowd, then runs. It's **the Caller's family**.

> CALLER (running): "I went somewhere! I went *really far*!"

They hug for a long time. Then the Caller's parent takes out an ink stamp and opens the Caller's lane book to the first page.

> CALLER'S PARENT (placeholder): "First voyage. And you came home."

*Stamp.* The Caller's very first mark: Viera's.

**[PLAY]** **Lane books.** One by one, each family member holds out their book: Earth on the first page, then every world they reached, then **Viera** stamped on the last. Bonus stamps from optional discoveries shine on the margins. *Ding.* And on the very last page of every book in Farport, a small printed star has appeared that nobody stamped.

> LUKE (flipping through his): "We've been *everywhere*."
>
> LYLA (pointing at the star): "Him."

**The passenger** flies down with the hatchlings and gives the kids a **keepsake**: a small glowing scale that **hums the Heartnote** when you hold it.

> LUNA (holding it to her ear): "It's singing *our* note."

### A3-11c — The Dragon Chooses

**[CINE]** The family walks back to their dragon. The star-dragons gather around it, and the Lanemother lowers her head beside it. On the edge of the field, the Forge's shipwrights who first drew its blueprints have come to look at it. Above them all, the Lodestar shines. The dragon was made in his image, on the world that made him. It could stay.

> CALLER: "It can stay, if it wants. Or go with you. It's its choice. Nobody gets to make it for the dragon."

The dragon looks at the star-dragons. It looks at the Lanemother, who lowers her head: *it's up to you*. It looks up at the Lodestar for a long time. Then it looks at the family.

It **lowers its wing** to the ground, like a ramp, for them to climb aboard.

> LUKE: "It picked us."
>
> MARIA: "It picked us back."

*This mirrors the family's own choice in Act I, and answers Morrow's: nobody was chosen, and everyone chose.*

### A3-11d — The Resting Chair

**[CINE]** The Rest Garden, now full of people for the first time in ages. NEIGHBOR unfolds her **lawn chair** next to the Hush's favorite bench, sits down, and closes her eyes. A Vieran traveler watches, puzzled, then carefully sits on the bench beside her. Then another. Soon there's a whole row of travelers, just sitting.

> TRAVELER (placeholder, whispering): "What are we doing?"
>
> NEIGHBOR (eyes still closed): "Resting. Before you go. You used to know that."

The Hush drifts down and leaves **a small, perfectly quiet pebble** in her lap. When she holds it, everything around her goes softly quiet for a moment. Not held. Just quiet. Then the sound comes back, the way it's supposed to.

> NEIGHBOR: "…*Oh*. Oh, that's the difference."

When the family turns to leave, she folds up her clipboard (returned, a little dusty, by the robot iguana) but leaves the chair.

> NEIGHBOR: "It's staying. They need it more than I do."
>
> *(beat)*
>
> NEIGHBOR: "Still not approving the dragon."

### A3-11e — The Lane Home

**[CINE]** Back at the dragon, Luna holds up the **Bridge**. The panel in the dragon's chest opens, and the Bridge slides into place with a click and a warm glow. The way across is whole again.

Then the Lanemother rises over Farport and sings. Not the SOS: a new song, long and steady. A **lane** of light unrolls from Viera's sky, past the Lodestar, out past the Mooring Ring, across the dark, toward a tiny blue dot.

> CALLER: "She's singing you a lane. To *your* home. It'll always be there."
>
> PAUL: "So we can go home…"
>
> CALLER: "…and come back. Fair lanes, both ways."

This keeps Act I's promise of a way home. **[STATE]** `bridge_restored = true`, `lane_to_earth = true`

### A3-11f — Hold Them

**[CINE]** Before the family climbs aboard, the Lanemother lowers her great head to Paul and Maria. She looks at the kids, then at the star that used to be her son, then back at the parents. She speaks, and they understand her without any help at all.

> LANEMOTHER: "Hold them."
>
> *(beat)*
>
> LANEMOTHER: "And then let them go."
>
> *(beat)*
>
> LANEMOTHER: "Both. That is the whole song."

Maria takes Paul's hand. Neither of them says anything.

### A3-11g — Closing Image

**[CINE]** Night on Viera. The family is on a blanket in the Rest Garden, kids half-asleep, with the dragon curled around them like a cat. Travelers rest on the benches. The Sweeper hums softly in the harbor. **[MUSIC]** The music-box family theme, now with the Heartnote, and under it, high and pure, the Lodestar's tone.

Overhead, the **Lodestar** shines, still and bright. Beside it, the **lane home to Earth** glows steady among the stars.

The dragon hums the SOS, softly, all seven notes, then rests, then home.

And from far, far away, down the lane, **faint voices hum back.**

**Cut to black.**

## Post-credits — Block Party

**[CINE]** The cul-de-sac at dusk, some time later. The dragon is curled around the house. The family is home for the weekend, and **the whole neighborhood is out**:
- BAKER NEIGHBOR with **the biggest cake yet**, shaped like the family riding the dragon.
- TRUCK NEIGHBOR: "Lawn's mowed!"
- TOOLS NEIGHBOR: "Back in my day… no. Nothing in my day was like this."
- TEACHER: "Did you bring me a picture?" Lyla gives her an archive card. *Ding.*

**[MUSIC]** The **family theme, with the Heartnote**.

A **small Vieran ship** comes down the lane of light and lands softly on the street. The hatch opens. It's **the Caller**, on their second voyage, holding a gift wrapped in Vieran cloth: a cake-sized pastry for the baker neighbor.

> CALLER: "Fair lanes!"
>
> LUNA (running): "You came!"

Across the street, NEIGHBOR is on her porch in a brand-new lawn chair. She raises a hand, briefly. On her windowsill: the Hush's quiet pebble, next to a repaired birdbath and a single star-shaped cookie.

> NEIGHBOR: "…Goodnight."

Above the cul-de-sac, very faint, if you know where to look: one new star that doesn't twinkle.

**Cut to black.**

## After the ending (the post-game, proposed)

- **The lanes stay open.** Free travel between every world, Earth and Viera included, with lane-book stamps left to find. This reconciles with v0.1's canon C02, "explores the universe," as a **post-game mode** that only arrives after the SOS has been answered. That keeps Act I's rule: no sightseeing campaign between assembly and answering.
- **Five Powers resolves** to the Heartnote everywhere ([music doc](../MUSIC_EMPATHY_AND_PACING.md) §2.2).
- **Wingmen** can be called on flights: the star-dragons, the hatchlings, or the Sweeper as a very slow tow.
- **Open hooks** (OPEN): a second power per family member; another call from another universe; the Hush visiting Earth for some peace and quiet *in the neighbor's garden*; and the Lodestar, which on certain nights, very faintly, flickers.
