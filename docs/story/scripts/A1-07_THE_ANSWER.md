# Script — A1-07 and A1-08: The Answer (Act I finale)

**Status:** PROPOSED draft. Conventions follow [First Contact](A1-00_FIRST_CONTACT.md).  
**Canon this must keep ([Act I](../ACT_I_BUILD_THE_DRAGON.md) §5):**
- The dragon is built from the **pre-existing design**, and the family didn't invent or redesign it.
- The full SOS explains the journey **before** they decide.
- **No secret abduction**, and no irreversible departure hidden behind a "play" button.
- They **decide together**.
- Act I ends with the dragon complete, the purpose understood, and the family committed.

## A1-07 — Assembly Complete

**[CINE]** The backyard at dusk. The family carries the Bridge piece across the street, and NEIGHBOR follows a few steps behind, "just to make sure it doesn't break anything else." The whole street is out. **[MUSIC]** The family theme, slow and expectant.

Luna holds the Bridge up to the dragon's chest. **It reaches for her**, the living material curling out like a hand, and **takes it in.**

**[CINE]** The canon staging:
- The five suits light up, and each family member glows in their own color. That's the final calibration.
- The Core projects the **complete stored design**, the whole dragon at last. It's the same design it showed the first night, finally whole.
- The Builder Bugs swarm. Sections unfold and connect. The spine straightens, the legs settle, and the **wings open above the cul-de-sac.**
- **Five seats light up** inside its chest.
- **The dragon's eyes open.** They stay open.

It looks at each family member in turn, then up at the sky where the fading star blinks. It makes **one sound**: low, curious, like a question. Its personality is OPEN, and this single sound is enough for now.

> LUKE (whispering): "Hi."

The street erupts in cheering. The BAKER NEIGHBOR's dragon cake finally gets eaten. Across the walk, NEIGHBOR stares up at the open wings.

> NEIGHBOR (quietly): "…It really can fly, can't it."

## A1-07b — First Flight (flight school and the send-off dogfight)

**[CINE]** The Core chimes. Its sentences are short and clear now:

> CORE: "Dragon ready. Test first. Then message."
>
> PAUL: "A test flight. Around the neighborhood. Everybody in."

**[PLAY]** **Pick a flight level** with pictures and spoken labels. This is an action-assistance setting, separate from the learner profile, and it can be changed any time.

| Level | Picture | Spoken label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cadet | A dragon with a star | "Easy flying — the dragon helps!" |
| Pilot | A dragon with two stars | "Real flying." |
| Ace | A dragon with three stars | "Full control." |

The default is **Cadet**. A parent can change it.

**[PLAY]** **Takeoff.** The dragon hovers straight up off the cul-de-sac. In Cadet it's guided: just hold Up. **[MUSIC]** The dragon horn plus the family theme at full orchestra. Neighbors wave from below, and porch lights shrink away.

**[PLAY]** **Flight school** ([combat system](../../gameplay/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md) §6). The Builder Bugs fly little target drones and light rings over the neighborhood. It teaches steering, Breath Cannon, Boost and, at Pilot and above, Seeker Sparks and flares, **one at a time, with no failure**. Lyla's archive cards pop up for each skill, as pictures only.

**[PLAY]** **The send-off dogfight (optional, recommended).**

> NEIGHBOR (on the ground, waving a gooey remote control): "I can't turn them OFF!"

Her **Mosquito Squadron**, programmed weeks ago to "remove the dragon," launches on autopilot. The Breath Cannon and Seeker Sparks disable them, and **each one pops a tiny parachute** and drifts down into her yard. An optional **Swamp Blimp** joins (the squadron's carrier), and the HUD marks its weak points. There are no crew roles: the pilot does it all. The dragon never crashes ([combat system](../../gameplay/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md) §6, Bonked).

> NEIGHBOR (as parachutes rain onto her lawn): "Sorry! Sorry! I'm *sorry*!"

*Act II seed:* a few drones fly off on autopilot toward the fading star, possibly the "leftover drones" in Act II.

**[PLAY]** **Landing.** The dragon hovers back down onto the cul-de-sac. Cadet is guided. Pilot and Ace can try an optional manual landing "down the street runway."

## A1-08 — The Full Message

**[CINE]** Night. The dragon is curled in the cul-de-sac with its chest glowing softly. The family sits together on the blanket by the Core, and the passenger (if home) is in Luna's lap. **[MUSIC]** Silence.

The Core turns to Lyla.

> CORE: "Lyla. Archive. Play."

**[PLAY]** **Lyla plays the full message.** This is her hero beat.
- **Prompt:** "Lyla, play the whole message."
- One tap. **[HINT 2]** LYLA: "Me? …Okay!"

**[CINE]** The SOS plays **complete for the first time**, image and voice together, translated by the Core in short sentences. **[MUSIC]** The full arrangement, all seven notes, **ending on its question.**

> CORE (translating, over images of a fading, silent sky): "We need help. Anyone who can hear. Our world is going quiet. This dragon can come to us. **This dragon can bring you home.**"

The image shows the **other universe**, the opening (the fading star) as a ring, and a sleeping star-dragon. Then **a path back**: the dragon returning to a small blue planet. The promise of a way home is shown on screen, so the departure is never a secret.

The passenger hears it, sits up and **sings back**: a few notes, wobbly, longing. It wants to go home.

The family is quiet.

> ★ LUKE: "They sent all this because they need somebody?"
>
> ★ PAUL: "Yes."
>
> ★ LUKE: "And it came to us?"
>
> ★ MARIA: "It did."
>
> LYLA: "…Why us?"
>
> MARIA: "Not *why* us, sweetheart. It just came. What we do now is up to us."

## A1-08b — The Decision

**[PLAY]** **Hands on the Core.** Each family member places a hand on the Core: one tap per person, or one tap "all together." **[MUSIC]** A single instrument joins with each hand: brass, cello, strings, marimba, celesta.

Then the choice, as two big pictures with spoken labels:

| Choice | Picture | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Answer the call** | The dragon flying toward a ring of light | → the goodbye |
| **Not yet** | The dragon curled up at home | Free play in the cul-de-sac. Fly around the neighborhood, replay missions, visit neighbors, flight school. **The fading star never closes.** Return to the Core any time to choose again. |

There's no penalty and no time limit. Choosing "Not yet" is never treated as wrong.

**[STATE]** `act1_decision = answer` (it's set only on Answer and is idempotent). **[SAVE cp_a1_decided]**

## A1-08c — The Goodbye

**[CINE]** Morning. The whole cul-de-sac sees them off.

Last, NEIGHBOR crosses the street with her arms folded.

> NEIGHBOR: "Somebody has to keep an eye on things."

*(An Act II seed: she means the street. Later she'll mean something else. She stows away.)* Nobody notices her glance at the dragon's open storage hatch.

## A1-08d — Liftoff (closing image)

**[PLAY]** One last input, which is the commitment: **"Take off!"** Hold Up, or tap Enter in Cadet.

**[CINE]** The dragon rises off the cul-de-sac. The house shrinks, then the street, then the town and the coastline. **[MUSIC]** The family theme becomes the SOS, played by everyone together, with all seven notes, still ending on its question.

Up ahead, the **fading star** grows bright, and then it **opens into a ring of light.**

**Cut to black.**

## Writing notes

- **The decision is real.** "Not yet" exists so that "Answer" means something. It also protects a child who isn't ready to "leave home," even in a game.
- **Home is promised before departure:** the message shows the way back. That keeps Act I's rule against hidden departures.
- **Five instruments, no home note.** The chord the family makes with their hands on the Core is still unresolved. The resolution waits for Act II's Home Note.
