## The owner's new direction

Four rules from October 29 split the off-world story into two acts and move the ending from the cul-de-sac to Viera.

| The owner said | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Stay linear | One fixed route. The "any order" missions and worlds get a set order, and beats can be written for a specific place instead of a count. |
| A large 3D world or set of worlds | Each planet becomes a big explorable place, not the "one compact area" the Act II drafts assume. |
| Three acts: Earth first, then the dragon carries us planet to planet | The drafted Act II holds the whole off-world trip. It needs to become Act II and Act III. |
| The final landing is on planet Viera, where everyone is a space traveler | Viera is a new place with no story yet. The drafted ending, flying home to a block party, no longer closes the game. |

The owner's words are preserved in the [idea inbox](../../ideas/INBOX.md) and recorded in the [decision](../decisions/2026-10-29-three-acts-linear-and-viera.md).

## Earlier proposed beats to fit into the current campaign

Use the canonical slot structure above. The following earlier fixed-route outline supplies proposed beat material; one numbered scene is not automatically a full mission. The Hush climax remains a proposed Viera payoff, and the family moving to Viera is confirmed. Names, individual missions and the placement of waypoint settings still need mapping into the five missions on each planet.

### Act I — Build the Dragon (Earth)

*Question: will we help?* Keep the drafts as written, in this order:

1. First Contact: living room, backyard Core, five suits, town map
2. Preschool: Assembly module
3. Luke's school: Navigation module (the fading star appears)
4. Warehouse store: Power module
5. Grocery: Life Support module (the passenger)
6. Big-box store: Wing Control module (the Core's symbol inside an iguana)
7. Rocket facility: Propulsion module (the dragon stands but won't wake)
8. Across the Street: the Bridge piece, won with kindness
9. The Answer: assembly, first flight, full message, the choice, liftoff

**Ends:** the dragon rises off the cul-de-sac and the ring opens.

### Act II — Answer the Call (the Quiet)

*Question: how do we help?* Mostly the drafted Act II, in this order:

1. The Crossing: light speed, then the universe crossing; the stowaway is found
2. The Last Beacon: meet the Caller, a young Vieran
3. Market Moon (Paul and Luna)
4. Hatchery Nebula (Maria and Lyla): the passenger finds its family, the emotional midpoint
5. Climbing Spires (Luke)
6. Forge Ring (everyone): the blueprints, the Song-breath upgrade

**Ends:** with four star-dragons awake, the last lane lights up on the star map. It points to Viera, dark at the center of everything, ringed by thousands of sleeping ships. The Caller: "That's home."

### Act III — The Road to Viera

*Question: where do travelers belong?* New writing, plus the Silent Center scripts moved here:

1. The Waystation (placeholder): a rest-stop world where travelers fell asleep mid-trip
2. The Drift (placeholder): a lane jammed with sleeping ships, a big flight and escort level
3. Arrival at Viera: the dragon falls asleep and lands in a silent spaceport city, the low point
4. Listen: the Hush, curled around Viera's Elder Dragon
5. Shelter: protect the Hush from the noise of a waking universe
6. Heartnote: the family sings the note the SOS never had
7. Viera wakes: every traveler launches at once; the Encore flight with the whole fleet
8. Final landing on Viera: the homecoming, the dragon's choice, the family made travelers

**Ends:** on Viera, with the ring home to Earth glowing in its sky.

## Narrative holes and proposed fills

Twelve holes, biggest first. The top four decide the shape of Acts II and III; the rest can be filled while writing. The [treatment](ACTS_II_III_THE_ROAD_TO_VIERA.md) develops each fill.

### Structure and Viera

1. **The off-world story is one act, not two.**
    - Fill: split it at the Forge Ring. Act II ends when the lane to Viera lights up. Act III is the road to Viera and the Hush climax there.
2. **Viera has no story.** Who lives there, and why is it the last stop?
    - Fill: Viera is the Caller's home and the hub where every travel lane meets. Everyone there is a space traveler, and the Quiet caught them all asleep mid-journey. That's why they needed outsiders: anyone inside the Quiet falls asleep.
3. **Nothing explains why the route is linear.**
    - Fill: lanes. Each star-dragon the family wakes sings open the lane to the next world. The star map only shows lit lanes, and every lane ends at Viera. The order comes from the story, not an invisible wall.
4. **Why the Quiet happened is open.** The Hush notes say the dragons stopped taking rests, but not why.
    - Fill: Viera's travelers kept going farther and faster, and the star-dragons sang the lanes nonstop for them. Nobody came home to rest, so the Hush, the rest in the song, got squeezed out. It's nobody's fault, which matches the neighbor's yard in Act I. Act III's lesson: even travelers need a home.

### Ending and home

5. **Home or Viera?** The drafts end in the cul-de-sac and promise a way home.
    - Fill: the last scene is on Viera, where the family is made honorary travelers. The ring home stays open in Viera's sky, which keeps the Act I promise. A short trip home for the block party becomes an optional epilogue or post-game.
6. **Act III has no planets yet.**
    - Fill: two waypoints, then Viera as the biggest world. The Waystation is on foot (sleeping travelers at a rest stop, an airport kids recognize). The Drift is a flight level (escorting sleeping ships out of a jam).

### Characters

7. **The neighbor's arc has no Act III.** She stows away in the drafts, and her quiet lesson reaches the Hush.
    - Fill: she rides all the way to Viera. That climax beat moves with the Hush, and she becomes the first to teach the travelers to rest.
8. **The Caller needs an arc beyond "sent the SOS".**
    - Fill: the Caller is the one Vieran too young for a first voyage. The trip to Viera aboard the family's dragon is that first voyage.
9. **The new cast has no source.** Mox, Tavi, Captain Nera and Rook likely fit Act III, but their file is missing.
    - Fill: get the file before assigning roles. Act III has open slots for a traveler crew and a fleet captain.
10. **The dragon's choice and the Core's homecoming.** Viera's people designed both.
    - Fill: at the final landing the dragon is home among its makers and the star-dragons, and still lowers its wing for the family. Whether the Core stays on Viera is open.

### Play

11. **Big worlds would feel empty.** Everyone in the Quiet is asleep.
    - Fill: waking spreads zone by zone. Sound brings color back, and each restored zone wakes its locals, who become guides and side-quest givers. Every world gets louder and fuller as you play.
12. **No real opponent off Earth.** Two acts of dogfights only have Hush-moths and sleepwalking sentries.
    - Fill, option A: no villain. Threats grow as the Quiet thickens near Viera: moth swarms, sleepwalking defenses, drifting ships.
    - Fill, option B: a fictional rival who wants the sleeping ships, possibly from the missing character file.

Smaller fixes: lock the Act I order shown above, seed Viera early (the recurring symbol becomes the travelers' mark, and the full message ends on a ship-ringed planet), and add traveler stamps from each world that pay off on Viera. Act III also needs one paused, assisted learning challenge per world.
