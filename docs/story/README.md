## The story at a glance

**Premise (CANON).** An SOS from another universe sends rescue equipment across the gap. Earth was in range, and the family wasn't chosen. *"We weren't chosen. We were in range. Then we chose to help."*

**The villain (CANON direction; details PROPOSED).** The SOS was sent because of **Morrow, the Long Note**: the Lanemother's chosen firstborn, who couldn't bear for anything to leave, so he held the **Heartnote**, the note every song comes home to, and stopped a whole universe mid-moment. He was chosen; the family wasn't. See the [villain arc](VILLAIN_ARC.md).

**Crew (CANON).**

| Who | Suit |
| --- | --- |
| Paul | Armor in the style of Iron Man |
| Maria | Purple healer |
| Luke | Suit in the style of Iron Spider |
| Luna | Pink, slippery goo |
| Lyla | Information gathering and storage. Color OPEN. |

The suits and the dragon are made of **living material** (OWNER IDEA).

**Act I.**
- It opens with a living-room computer interruption and a backyard crash (CANON).
- The suits form around helpful actions (PROPOSED).
- The family uses suits to recover dragon parts around town (CANON). Six modules and a note restored per component are proposed treatment details; the Earth mission count remains OPEN. The compact neighborhood may use the [owner-approved real public landmarks](../world/NEIGHBORHOOD_MAP.md). Six restored musical phrases are a proposed way to foreshadow Morrow's confirmed hold on the Heartnote.
- The neighbor escalates with swamp-tech (CANON).
- A seventh piece, the Bridge, landed in her yard. Kindness, not force, wins it back (PROPOSED).
- The dragon is assembled, the full message plays, and the family chooses to go (CANON).

**Structure (CANON).** Earth uses the suits to recover dragon parts and build the dragon. Dragon travel then connects four exploration planets in other universes, with dogfights interrupting travel, followed by fifth/final planet Viera. Every off-Earth planet has five missions: **20 before Viera + five on Viera = 25**, plus the Earth opening. The family moves to Viera. The route remains linear across three acts. Planet names/content and universe assignments remain open/proposed; see [the campaign](CAMPAIGN_STRUCTURE.md).

**Mission party (CANON).** At most three family members participate in an active mission. One is directly controlled and one is a mostly autonomous sidekick with a sidekick button. An optional third member's exact support/control behavior is open. Five family members remain the cast; older all-five mission gates must be adapted rather than treated as exceptions.

**Act II — four planets × five missions and the Morrow arc CONFIRMED; staging/mechanics PROPOSED.**
- The four planets supply 20 mission slots. Market Moon, Hatchery, Climbing Spires and Forge Ring remain proposed names.
- A universe is held still inside Morrow's note, the Quiet, and only outsiders can still move and make sound.
- The family meets the Caller, hears the Old Song, and wakes star-dragons on four worlds in a fixed order. Each one lights the song-lane to the next.
- The Sweeper, a lane-keeper robot Morrow has turned into his hands, is collecting every ship to Viera; its drones are the dogfight opponents.
- At the Forge, Morrow comes down the last lane and makes the family an offer. They refuse. The neighbor accepts, and takes the Bridge, the way home, with her.

**Act III — five Viera missions, relocation and the Morrow arc CONFIRMED; staging/mechanics PROPOSED.**
- Viera is the fifth and final planet. Waystation/Drift ideas fit existing slots or travel; they do not add a sixth planet.
- With no way home, the family flies the last lane through the Sweeper's fleet, re-tuning it at the Mooring Ring.
- Their dragon, built in Morrow's likeness, hears him call it *brother* and goes still on landing. The family crosses a Farport frozen mid-goodbye on foot, and gets suit upgrades.
- In the Rest Garden, Morrow lies coiled over his old seal. The neighbor turns back. The Hush is understood (Listen) and protected (Shelter), and the family's Heartnote, followed by a rest, breaks his note.
- Upgrades become powers. In the Last Hold, Morrow lets go of the Heartnote and becomes the Lodestar. The family lands on Viera for good. A post-credits scene returns to the cul-de-sac.

The retained [planet-missions source](PLANET_MISSIONS_AND_SOS_COMPOSITION.md) proposes Whisperwood/Tideglass, Stillheart and named-character arcs. Its composition idea is partly incorporated in the current Act II/III treatments, while its selectable middle group and Stillheart/Earth ending are superseded by confirmed linear/Viera direction. Candidate worlds and Tavi/Mox/Nera/Rook beats remain proposals; the named companion file remains absent from this checkout. See [the updated filing receipt](../reports/2026-10-29-planet-missions-filing.md) before combining the drafts. None of this later campaign is implemented.

**Play (direction CANON, details PROPOSED).**
- On foot, suits plus alley-oops.
- The dragon flies like a fighter jet, with one pilot and no crew roles.
- Keyboard first, with an Xbox controller supported if it works with no setup.
- No game over, no gore, and help never blocks the story.
