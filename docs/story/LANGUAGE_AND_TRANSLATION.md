# Language

**Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Status:** CONFIRMED owner direction (SD-037, [decision](../decisions/2026-10-30-translation.md)): **the game is in English**, and the suits are a hand-wave for why the family can understand everyone. It isn't a mechanic. The flavor details below are PROPOSED.  
**Source:** owner, Space Dragon chat, October 30, 2026: *"We need: a way to translate language,"* then *"The suits can translate,"* then *"The game just needs to be in English with the suit core hand waiving why we can understand."*

## The rule

- **Everything is in English:** every character, sign, screen and line, on Earth and off it, Vierans and Morrow included.
- **The suits are why.** They're made of the same living material as the Core, and they just make it work. That's the whole explanation.
- **It's said once,** when the family meets the Caller at Lantern Rock ([script](scripts/A2-00_THE_CROSSING.md)), and never again:

> LUKE: "Wait. How can we understand you?"
>
> CALLER: "...How can I understand *you*?"
>
> MARIA (tapping the cuff of her suit): "I think it's these."
>
> LUKE: "Cool."

- **No mechanic:** no translation meter, no alien script to decode, no mistranslations, no words dropping out.
- **The neighbor** has no suit and understands everyone anyway. Nobody asks.

## What stays

- **Vieran flavor, in English.** Vierans greet each other with "Fair lanes!" The Caller explains that it means hello, good luck and be safe out there.
- **The Heartnote symbol.** The recurring symbol from the first transmission in Act I is the Heartnote's mark, a small circle with a line curving back into it, on the Core, the pods, the dragon's chest and every lane book.
- **The suits shiver when Morrow speaks.** It's not about language: they're made of his song and know their oldest voice.
- **Creatures don't talk.** Star-dragons, hatchlings and the passenger sing and act. Lyla's visor can show a picture of what they want: *follow me*, *danger*, *thank you*. The Lanemother talks.
- **The Core's Act I arc** of finding its voice as it's repaired ([Act I refinements](ACT_I_CRITIQUE_AND_REFINEMENTS.md) §3.2) is a companion arc, not a translation system, and is unaffected.

## Learning uses real English

Signs, cards, docking bays and departure boards in the other universe use plain English letters, numbers and words. That way the [curriculum puzzles](../learning/CURRICULUM_PUZZLE_PLAN.md) practice exactly what the kids are learning at school:

- the word cards from each star-dragon's waking: **HELLO**, **FRIEND**, **HOME**, **REST**;
- the lettered stalls on the Market Moon and the numbered nests in the Hatchery;
- the Big Board in Farport's Departure Halls;
- **MORROW**, the name on the Forge blueprint, which Luke sounds out himself when the Core won't say it.

## Retired

Earlier drafts on October 30 gave the other universe a sung language, a drawn script decoded in a "Glyph Book," two-way translation in instrument voices, mistranslations, words dropping out near Morrow, the Caller filling gaps, the robot iguana translating for the neighbor, and a "gift of tongues" when Morrow lets go. All of that is retired by the owner's direction.
