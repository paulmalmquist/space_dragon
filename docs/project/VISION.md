Paul supplies creative ideas without organizing technical requirements. Agents preserve those thoughts, connect them to decisions, maintain dependencies, make routine engineering choices, and show a playable result that can be adjusted.

## Creative foundation and authority

**Latest confirmed structure — October 30:** [the campaign contract](../story/CAMPAIGN_STRUCTURE.md) and [decision](../decisions/2026-10-30-campaign-and-mission-party.md) govern older treatments. Earth uses the suits to recover parts and assemble the dragon. The dragon then carries the family to four exploration planets in other universes, five missions each, with travel interrupted by dogfights. Viera is the fifth and final off-Earth planet, also with five missions, and the family moves there: **25 off-Earth missions plus the Earth opening**. The first four planet names, detailed missions and universe assignments remain open/proposed.

**Mission party:** at most three family members; one is directly controlled and one is a mostly autonomous sidekick with a sidekick button. An optional third member's exact support/control behavior remains open. The five-person family cast does not require five participants in every mission. Earlier five-person mission gates and extra full-planet routes must be adapted to this contract.

The [Act I story](../story/ACT_I_BUILD_THE_DRAGON.md) remains the canonical opening: living-room computer, interrupted signal, backyard arrival and suits, scattered town components, assembly of a pre-existing dragon design, and a choice to answer an SOS in another universe. The equipment was deliberately sent, reached this family accidentally, and did not select them in advance. They did not invent the dragon.

The [story index](../story/README.md) and [production scripts](../story/scripts/README.md) organize the later writing. The owner approved [Morrow and his arc](../story/VILLAIN_ARC.md) as the reason for the SOS: his held note creates the Quiet, the neighbor accepts his offer and takes the Bridge, returns in the Rest Garden, and Morrow ends as the Lodestar (SD-065–066). **Heartnote** is the proposed replacement for the retired term “home note”; the owner requested the rename but has not separately approved this replacement word. Dialogue, mechanics, appearance and other names retain their proposal status. The selected First Contact implementation does not imply that these later story beats are implemented.

**Confirmed language direction — SD-069:** the game is in English, including signs and learning prompts. The suits supply a one-line explanation for mutual understanding; translation is not a gameplay mechanic. [Language](../story/LANGUAGE_AND_TRANSLATION.md) retires glyph decoding, dropped words and translation meters while keeping the Core's separate companion arc.

The root `new characters` file is still a download-note stub. The filed [planet-missions/composition proposal](../story/PLANET_MISSIONS_AND_SOS_COMPOSITION.md) supplies proposed Tavi/Mox/Captain Nera/Rook roles, dialogue and Hush clues, plus a six-world composition campaign. Its named companion `SPACE_DRAGON_NEW_CHARACTERS_AND_STORY_BEATS.md` remains absent from this checkout. Preserve the source as a proposal; its selectable middle group and Stillheart/Earth ending do not override the confirmed linear/Viera direction below. The incoming Act II/III treatments already incorporate collaborative composition in their proposed route. [The reconciliation receipt](../reports/2026-10-29-planet-missions-filing.md) tracks remaining differences; Caller/passenger names are not silently replaced and Rook is not the Sweeper.

**Confirmed later gameplay direction:** on-foot tandems are family alley-oops; dragon flight has a fighter-jet/simulator feel with one pilot and no crew roles. [The gameplay decision](../decisions/2026-10-29-alley-oops-and-jet-flight.md) owns that direction. Detailed combat, assist tiers and timing remain proposed and outside First Contact.

**Confirmed story structure (October 29):** the story is linear across a large world or set of worlds, in three acts. Act I is on Earth; then the dragon carries the family planet to planet, and the final landing is on planet Viera, where everyone is a space traveler. Suits grow from upgrades into powers by the end, and the dragon dogfights and navigates asteroid fields between planets. See [the decision](../decisions/2026-10-29-three-acts-linear-and-viera.md). The [three-act plan](../story/THREE_ACT_PLAN.md) and [Acts II & III treatment](../story/ACTS_II_III_THE_ROAD_TO_VIERA.md) supply proposed staging around the confirmed campaign and Morrow arc; the Sweeper is his machine subordinate rather than the final villain.

**Confirmed curriculum focus (October 30):** numbers/letters for the girls and addition/subtraction/reading for Luke inform [the curriculum puzzle plan](../learning/CURRICULUM_PUZZLE_PLAN.md). The proposed puzzle tracks use a parent's local content list; no learning implementation or assessment claim follows from the plan.

## Current production direction

**Confirmed dragon art benchmark — SD-059:** a formidable, intelligent living machine protects the family, with gunmetal/dark painted primary armor, deep crimson secondary plates and restrained red-orange internal light. [The bounded production direction](../decisions/2026-10-30-cinematic-dragon-benchmark.md) starts with the head, neck and one shoulder, measured and exported into the real game before expanding the whole body. Believable construction, distinct materials and readable silhouette take priority over random spikes, excessive glow or uniform wear. This new candidate supersedes the earlier all-red palette; original blue/red versions remain for comparison and rollback. Cinematic/GTA V references are quality aspirations, not verified equivalence or a reason by themselves to change engines.

**Confirmed workflow — Seele prototypes:** use Seele to generate prototype games and combine selected systems, assets and game feel into one Space Dragon game. The first prototype track is dragon dogfighting (SD-036). The owner now requests the first import and meaningful local edits (SD-037): barrel rolls and independent W/A/D/Z aiming with arrow/mouse steering, left-button firing and right-button zoom. [The integration plan](../engineering/PROTOTYPE_INTEGRATION.md) and [scoped lab decision](../decisions/2026-10-30-seele-dogfight-lab.md) preserve the campaign, party/single-pilot rules, existing saves and browser target. The isolated Dogfight Lab retains its Three.js presentation while Earth stays Babylon; it is not yet a campaign mission or a whole-game renderer decision. Prototype order can begin with flight while the story still begins on Earth.

**Selected local foundation under the owner's setup authorization:** Babylon.js and TypeScript; Blender source assets with GLB runtime exports; a tested WebGL2 starting point; versioned local checkpoints; browser checks and screenshots. The engine choice is an implementation decision that can be revised if the existing-site audit or a concrete imported environment provides a strong reason. The October 30 [Seele migration direction](../../seeles-assets/README.md) explicitly considers early refactoring when it lowers total development effort. WebGPU and Havok are options to evaluate when a scene or mechanic needs them.

**Confirmed goal:** rich cinematic presentation, as close to AAA style as practical in a browser, with Railway intended for later backend services. Recognizable family characters, engineered armor, strong lighting, animation and sound should create quality. Current placeholders and a technical pod candidate are not that final visual standard.

**Confirmed quality emphasis:** the game must feel right, with AAA-like care in a bounded world rather than extensive customization or open-world scale (SD-022). [The game-feel bar](../engineering/GAME_FEEL.md) guides camera, movement, animation, interaction feedback, sound and responsiveness reviews. Technical navigation checks establish working controls, not this final quality standard.

**Confirmed world scale and anchors:** a small-neighborhood microcosm of Winston Trails/Lake Worth, with compressed connections to Wellington South Target, Home Depot at Lantana/Jog, Lake Worth Christian, Manatee Elementary and a few nearby restaurants (SD-024). Real place names and public landmarks are allowed. [The map plan](../world/NEIGHBORHOOD_MAP.md) separates those confirmed anchors from its proposed layout. Google Maps reference logging is authorized (SD-025). The Earth walking slice retains its generic room/yard; the separate [Town Drive Lab](../engineering/ARCHITECTURE.md) now supplies a mapped Starpass exit, neighborhood driving, traffic, pedestrians and an optional minivan cabin. That lab is not the full campaign town or its mission system.

The owner also supplied a home anchor and requested the surrounding streets and nearby lake (SD-026). Keep the exact location in local reference notes while using it to inform the compressed residential/lake setting. Actual home footage has not arrived.

**Resources and hardware:** the owner reports an available Railway backend and willingness to connect cloud compute if useful (SD-023); actual service/project access has not been inspected. Keep this Lenovo with Chrome as the initial target. A replacement computer is not planned now. Use measured bottlenecks to justify cloud production work, runtime changes or a later hardware discussion.

**Authorized increment:** follow the First Contact script through living-room signal, backyard, five placeholder suit introductions, Lyla's supported scan, symbol archive and town map. It should work without reading through spoken placeholder prompts, pictured targets, Kid Keys and an escalating help path. Assisted completion advances the same story; the scan is not a reading assessment or mastery claim. Checkpoints and one-time rewards belong to a pure state module.

**Retained Earth increment — walking:** a visible provisional person explores the living room and backyard with a third-person camera, collisions and nearby interactions. A practice pickup and position save live independently of First Contact rewards. This reversible movement slice is explicitly authorized; it does not wait for the earlier story's human playtest. The owner also wants video of the actual house, yard and garage to inform a later environment pass. [The video idea](../REFERENCE_HOME_VIDEO.md) is recorded, but the current layout is generic and the garage is a backdrop.

**Confirmed first target:** this Lenovo 82XM with Chrome, on keyboard. In walking mode WASD/arrows move, drag or Q/E turns the camera, R recenters, and Space/Enter interacts nearby. In the story, arrows point and Space/Enter acts. Optional Xbox and [PlayStation controller support](../decisions/2026-10-30-playstation-controller.md) retains keyboard completion; physical pairing and family play remain separate checks. Touch is deferred. Human movement feel and the ten-minute family story playtest, including audibility/help points, remain review gates; automated evidence does not replace them.

**Engineering split:** TypeScript/Babylon runs the Earth slice; the isolated SD-037 Dogfight Lab retains its editable JavaScript/Three.js export. Python runs small repository/asset tools. The owner's Rust/C++ question is not a request to rewrite the game. A Rust/C++ WebAssembly module can be evaluated later for a measured compute bottleneck; changing tooling language alone does not improve visuals. WebGPU and Havok also await a concrete measured need.

**Asset proof:** the original rescue-pod candidate passes Blender → GLB → ETC1S KTX2 → actual browser loading with preserved lid/clip names and self-hosted decoding. [Its receipt](../reports/2026-10-29-pod-asset.md) records technical results. The Dogfight dragon now has a [verified local pipeline](../engineering/DRAGON_ASSET_CONTRACT.md) with preserved source/rollback, Blender clips, named attachments, separate collision and measured browser comparisons (SD-060). Neither technical result supplies human art approval; family characters still need their reference and likeness reviews.

The player's browser renders and runs the game. Keep the planned `paulmalmquist.com/space-dragon` prefix and existing homepage when integration becomes possible. Actual website source/routing/deployment and Railway services remain unverified. Local saves come first; backend expansion needs a concrete requirement.
