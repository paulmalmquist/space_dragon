## Bounded increment and superseded direction

Upgrade the existing dragon's **head, neck and one shoulder first**, then export that section in a complete playable candidate and inspect it in Dogfight Lab before extending the treatment to the rest of the body. This is the owner's chosen visual benchmark, not authorization to finish every part of the dragon in this pass. Keep the recognizable original proportions available for comparison.

The new candidate uses **gunmetal/dark painted primary armor, deep crimson secondary plates and restrained red-orange internal light**. This supersedes [SD-050's all-red treatment](2026-10-30-red-mechanical-dragon.md) for the new benchmark, and authorizes the bounded shape/material redesign that [SD-049's original integration](2026-10-30-mechanical-dragon-integration.md) excluded. Preserve the supplied blue v002 source, the optimized red source and the red/organic runtime models as historical baselines and rollback. Do not recolor or overwrite those originals.

The dragon is a powerful living machine that protects the family: formidable, intelligent and capable of spaceflight. Mechanical construction and readable silhouette should carry that impression. Avoid arbitrary spikes, excessive emissive strips, uniformly noisy wear, pure-black surfaces that hide shapes, or a single metallic material across the body. GTA V and the supplied cinematic reference establish a quality aspiration; neither establishes measured performance or an engine-migration requirement.

## Production sequence

1. **Measure first.** Inspect current source/export geometry, materials, textures, skeleton and runtime cost. Confirm the actual chase/zoom camera and projected detail before choosing triangle or texture budgets. Proposed budgets must be distinguished from measured results.
2. **Preserve and block out.** Save a new versioned source, retain the original in a comparison collection, and build the upgrade in a new collection. Show neutral front, side and three-quarter primary-form views before adding fine detail. The requested first-section work emphasizes predatory brow, recessed eyes, cheek armor, deep articulated mouth, integrated swept-back horns, overlapping neck protection and a stronger shoulder with moving-part clearances.
3. **Build deliberate armor.** Layer plates with varied size and actual thickness, small highlight-catching bevels, recessed seams and intentional gaps. Put exposed mechanisms around articulation points. Use large armor masses, medium joint structures and restrained small details, with broad quiet areas. Preserve component identities, origins, pivots and usable articulation. A joint not present in the current rig is not a verified animation merely because plates leave clearance for it.
4. **Establish reusable materials.** Separate mostly nonmetallic dark paint, rich crimson paint, directional exposed metal, dark rough flexible joints, structured rib-supported wing membrane and restrained emissive machinery. Wear belongs selectively on edges, hinges, claws and contact surfaces. The six-role material set is the reusable direction; full wing/body remodeling remains a later pass.
5. **Prepare a separate game candidate.** Keep detailed source and optimized geometry distinct. UV unwrap with consistent density and more allocation for the visible head than rarely seen undersides. Bake normal/AO where appropriate; keep AO out of base color. Use glTF-reproducible channels, separate emission from bloom, preserve rig rest transforms and attachment structure, and exclude recovery geometry/review studio/reference images. Export to a fresh versioned GLB and ensure runtime material treatment retains authored maps.
6. **Compare and play.** Produce full-body and close head comparisons in neutral light and in controlled cinematic light with a large soft key, rim and restrained fill/reflections. Check red detail and dark-form readability. Load the candidate in the actual game; observe framing, flap/glide, rolls, jaw/tail, aiming, ordinary plasma, charged windup/roar/beam and muzzle alignment. Retain collision/crash/retry, reduced motion and save behavior; measure load/file/render cost on the named Lenovo/Chrome target.

The owner's full silhouette direction also calls for a stronger chest, narrower waist, structured wing shoulder/elbow/fingers, tapered tail and functional gripping/landing feet. Those remain the intended **later whole-body pass**, triggered only after the first-section export/browser benchmark exposes and resolves its material, rig and performance issues. This sequencing does not reject those requirements or quietly mark them complete.
