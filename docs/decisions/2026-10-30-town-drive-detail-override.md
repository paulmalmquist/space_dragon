The large original videos and intermediate reconstruction files may remain in local asset storage for size and workflow reasons; that is not a privacy or publication prohibition. Promoted runtime assets use the normal asset manifest. This override concerns Town Drive and its supplied references; unrelated family-photo/voice permissions, secrets and production deployment instructions are unchanged.

The original task and prior verification receipt remain historical evidence. Their superseded restrictions are not active instructions. Implementation and new verification are recorded in the [detail receipt](../reports/2026-10-30-town-drive-detail.md).
