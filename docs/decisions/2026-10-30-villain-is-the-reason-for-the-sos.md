# Decision — A villain is the reason for the SOS

**Recorded:** October 30, 2026
**Status:** CONFIRMED owner direction on the story's antagonist and tone. The villain's identity, lore and scenes are PROPOSED.
**Decision source:** the owner, in the Space Dragon chat: *"We need a villain arc, the reason for the SOS. Not kiddy please, this can be biblical or DC studios level."* Preserved in the [idea inbox](../../ideas/INBOX.md) as SD-065.

## Decision

1. **The story has a villain with a full arc.**
2. **The villain is the reason for the SOS.** The emergency the senders called for help with is the villain's doing.
3. **The villain arc is told at a mythic scale, not a kiddy one.** Biblical or DC Studios weight is the reference for tone.

## Scope and rationale

- The arc spans all three acts and resolves at or before the final landing on Viera ([three-act decision](2026-10-29-three-acts-linear-and-viera.md)).
- Act I canon is unchanged. The family still receives deliberately sent equipment by accident, was not chosen, and chooses to help.
- The confirmed non-lethal flight rules still hold: unmanned enemy aircraft and no fatal crashes ([flight decision](2026-10-29-alley-oops-and-jet-flight.md)). The learning rules and family safeguards in `AGENTS.md` are unchanged. The [villain treatment](../story/VILLAIN_ARC.md) proposes "mythic, not graphic" as the way to meet this direction for players aged three and five.
- Star Wars, Starfield, DC and biblical stories guide tone only. No names, characters or text are borrowed.

## Superseded guidance

These were proposals, now replaced by owner direction:

- "Nobody meant it" as the cause of the Quiet, in the [Acts II & III overview](../story/ACTS_II_III_THE_ROAD_TO_VIERA.md) and [Act III](../story/ACT_III_THE_ROAD_TO_VIERA.md) §2. Someone did.
- Option A of hole 12 in the [three-act plan](../story/THREE_ACT_PLAN.md): no villain.
- The Hush pulling the silence over the universe as the cause of the Quiet ([music doc](../story/MUSIC_EMPATHY_AND_PACING.md) §3). The Hush stays gentle and never secretly malicious; it is no longer the cause.
- The Sweeper as the story's main off-world antagonist. As drafted it is a machine, not the reason for the SOS.

## Not decided here

Who the villain is, their name, lore and look; the neighbor's role in the arc; which existing beats change; and how the arc ends. The [villain treatment](../story/VILLAIN_ARC.md) proposes Morrow, the Long Note, with an integration list; it stays PROPOSED until confirmed here.

No implementation scope is authorized by this record. Story order is not implementation order.

## Update — Morrow approved, and the Heartnote (October 30, 2026)

**Owner direction:** *"Yes I like it. 'Home note' should be called something else."* Given in reply to the [villain treatment](../story/VILLAIN_ARC.md) and its summary. Preserved in the [idea inbox](../../ideas/INBOX.md) as SD-066.

- **Morrow, the Long Note, is the villain**, with the arc as presented: the Lanemother's chosen firstborn who held the note every song comes home to; the Temptation at the Forge, where the neighbor takes his offer and the Bridge; her return in the Rest Garden; and the Lodestar ending. The family's dragon is his likeness.
- **"Home note" is renamed.** The term is retired. Its replacement, **the Heartnote**, is proposed and applied throughout the story documents; the owner can rename it again.
- The treatment was integrated into the act treatments and scripts the same day. Dialogue, mechanics, his look and every other name stay PROPOSED.
