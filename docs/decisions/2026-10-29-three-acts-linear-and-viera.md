# Decision — Linear three-act structure, ending on planet Viera

**Recorded:** October 29, 2026  
**Status:** CONFIRMED owner direction on story structure, the final destination, suit progression and between-planet flight. The treatment built on it stays PROPOSED.  
**Decision source:** the owner, in a Claude chat in the Space Dragon project, October 29, 2026. Source wording is preserved in the [idea inbox](../../ideas/INBOX.md).

## Decision

1. **The story is linear.** The game follows one fixed route. Missions and worlds are not chosen in a player-selected order.
2. **The play space is a large 3D world or a set of worlds.**
3. **There are three major acts.** Act I takes place on Earth. After it, the dragon lets the family travel from planet to planet.
4. **The final landing place is planet Viera, where everyone is a space traveler.**
5. **Suits progress from upgrades to powers.** Early progression is suit upgrades; by the end it is powers. Room for both is left for the arrival on Viera.
6. **The dragon dogfights on the way to planets and navigates asteroid fields.** Set pieces that would be at home in Star Wars and Starfield are a reference for the feel.

## Scope and rationale

- Act I canon is unchanged: the living-room opening, the accidental arrival of deliberately sent equipment, the suits from the backyard arrival, the pre-existing dragon design and the choice to help.
- The other-universe destination is kept. The proposed treatment places Viera and the planets of Acts II and III in the universe the SOS came from.
- The confirmed flight rules still hold: fighter-jet feel, one pilot, no crew roles, unmanned enemy aircraft and no fatal crashes ([flight decision](2026-10-29-alley-oops-and-jet-flight.md)). Star Wars and Starfield guide the feel only; no names, characters, ships or designs are borrowed.
- Powers follow the living-material guardrails in the [Act I refinements](../story/ACT_I_CRITIQUE_AND_REFINEMENTS.md) §3.4: they switch off on request, helmets open, and the suit never controls its wearer.
- The [learning rules](../learning/LEARNING_RULES.md) are unchanged. No upgrade or power replaces or gates a learning challenge.

## Superseded guidance

- The Act I refinements §4.1 ordering rule (missions 2–5 in any order, mission 6 after any four). Missions now follow a fixed order. Delivering beats by install count still works but is no longer needed for coherence.
- "Any order" for the Act II worlds in the [Act II proposal](../story/ACT_II_ANSWER_THE_CALL.md) §5 and [A2-03](../story/scripts/A2-03_WAKING_THE_WORLDS.md), and the "Any order" labels in the [scripts index](../story/scripts/README.md).
- The drafted Encore and Home coda as the end of the game. The game's final landing is on Viera. (On October 30 that script was rewritten as [The Final Landing](../story/scripts/A3-09_THE_FINAL_LANDING.md).)
- The Act II open question "What Act III, if any, would be." There is an Act III.
- The [combat system](../gameplay/COMBAT_SYSTEM.md) §8 constraint that no mission may require a launch unlocked in a different mission. With a fixed order, later missions may rely on earlier unlocks.
- "One compact explorable area" per world as the Act II default. Large worlds are the direction; shrinking a world remains a production option.

## Not decided here

The specific mission and world order, Viera's people and lore, where Act II ends, the antagonist off Earth, the neighbor's role in Acts II and III, whether the family returns home after Viera, the individual upgrades and powers, and all names. The [three-act plan](../story/THREE_ACT_PLAN.md) and the [Acts II & III treatment](../story/ACTS_II_III_THE_ROAD_TO_VIERA.md) propose answers; they stay PROPOSED until confirmed here.

No implementation scope is authorized by this record. Story order is not implementation order, and the [current milestone](../plans/CURRENT_MILESTONE.md) is unchanged.
