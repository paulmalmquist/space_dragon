# Gentler enemy standoff movement — SD-051

October 30, 2026 · **CONFIRMED owner direction**, retained in [the inbox](../../ideas/INBOX.md): “also make the enemies a little more stationary instead of flying into me”. During the active [mechanical integration](2026-10-30-mechanical-dragon-integration.md), replace the lab's head-on pursuit/flyby movement with steadier player-relative standoff positions and gentle lateral drift. Exact spacing/tuning is an implementation choice: the current station offsets are 260–460 meters forward plus lateral spacing, with actual three-dimensional range varying during play. Facing/aim may turn independently of hover movement. This supersedes only [SD-043's](2026-10-30-dogfight-evasion.md) pursuit presentation: enemy tracking, incoming fire, health/damage, word-charge rules, evasion energy/roll effects and campaign-save boundaries remain. Verify that targets stay readable and avoid ramming while still firing, reacting to motion/rolls and supporting aim/breath. [The combined receipt](../reports/2026-10-30-mechanical-dragon-integration.md) owns actual checks; human difficulty/feel and deployment approval remain separate.

**Fire opportunity compensation:** continuous standoff facing/range removes old flyby downtime. Retaining the 1.35-second fire interval unintentionally tripled firing uptime in a deterministic simulation. The integration therefore uses a 4.4-second interval while keeping three-shot bursts, the 0.75-second telegraph, seven-point projectile damage and the existing 0.65-second damage guard. This is reversible tuning to preserve active incoming fire without treating a movement refinement as authorization to triple pressure. The receipt retains the actual intermediate/final simulation results; neither establishes a human difficulty or device-performance pass.
