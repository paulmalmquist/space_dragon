# Dad's armor and weapons: black with red glowing trim, repulsors and a katana — SD-092

November 17, 2026. **OWNER DECISION, CONFIRMED: Dad's armor is always black with red glowing trim, and his weapons are, in the owner's words, "repulsors and samurai style katana with a red light saber on the ground".** How that sentence reads, how the weapons play, when he gets them and what they are called on screen are PROPOSED or OPEN. The placeholder figure was redrawn to match: browser lab IMPLEMENTED and inspected; Unity IMPLEMENTED, NOT TESTED. **No weapon can be used anywhere yet.**

> Always black with red glowing trim (think iron man with a Darth Vader theme)
>
> Weapons are repulsors and samurai style katana with a red light saber on the ground.

The owner sent this in reply to the four questions in [the Tunnel Run record](2026-11-17-tunnel-run-and-dads-flight.md). It answers the fourth, "Is black and red Dad's armor everywhere?", and adds his weapons. The other three questions are still open.

## What it settles

1. **Always black with red glowing trim.** Not only in the Tunnel Run. This supersedes the v0.1 candidate palette of red, graphite and gold, which was never locked, and the reading taken earlier the same day, in which whole plates of the placeholder were red.
2. **The feeling to aim for:** Iron Man's powered armor with a Darth Vader theme, dark and imposing. Both are inspirations; see the originality rule below.
3. **His weapons:** repulsors, and a samurai-style katana with a red light saber, on the ground.

## How the second sentence was read

It can be read more than one way, so the reading is stated rather than assumed silently.

- **Reading taken (PROPOSED):** repulsors are his ranged weapon wherever he is, in flight included. On foot he also carries **one** sword: a katana in shape, with a blade of red light.
- **Other readings:** a katana and a separate red light-sword, two blades for the ground; or repulsors only in the air and blades only on the ground.
- **What depends on it:** one sword or two, and whether repulsors work on foot. Nothing built depends on it yet. It is the first question below.

## What was done now

- The wording is in [the inbox](../../ideas/INBOX.md) and [the ledger](../project/IDEA_LEDGER.md) as SD-092.
- The suit document, the combat design, the Tunnel Run record, the sidekick record, the roadmap, the status page and the Unity runbook say what is now settled and what is open.
- **Dad's placeholder in Luke's Arena was redrawn**, in the browser lab and in the Unity runtime's code:
  - black armor, in two finishes so the plates still read at night;
  - red glowing trim: a line across the visor, rings round the shoulders, belt, arms and legs, lines on the chest, shins and soles;
  - a sheathed katana across his back with a red edge and a red pommel;
  - a red emitter in each hand.
- That change is presentation only. No rule, input, number, save or recorded run changed, and the rules' fingerprint is the same as before. **The sword cannot be drawn and the emitters do nothing by themselves.**

## What was not done, and why

**No weapon is usable.** There is no katana attack, no repulsor ability of its own and no flight. They are parked, for these reasons:

- [The current milestone](../plans/CURRENT_MILESTONE.md) asks that nothing more be built on the arena until someone has played it.
- How the weapons play is not decided: the reading above, whether the katana replaces Dragon Punch, and when he gets them.
- Dad is not a playable hero anywhere yet. In the arena he is Luke's sidekick. His weapons matter most when he is the one being controlled, and that is a slice of its own.

**No suit was designed.** The figure is still a placeholder built from simple shapes: helmeted, no face, not a likeness. A real suit is Blender work under the [handoff](../BLENDER_CHARACTER_HANDOFF.md) and the [asset pipeline](../ASSET_PIPELINE.md), and the originality rule above applies to it.

**Revisit when** the owner asks for Dad's weapons to be playable, Dad becomes a playable leader in a lab, the Tunnel Run is taken up, or a Blender pass on his suit begins.

**The first slice worth building then** (PROPOSED): Dad as the controlled hero in the arena's existing ring, with Luke as his sidekick. One button fires a repulsor that stuns at range; the other is a single announced cut that powers down the robot it is aimed at. Same rules as today: nobody is hurt, nothing can be lost, nothing needs reading. *Proof: rule tests, a real-input browser test, the same results in C#, and a person playing it.*

## Questions only the owner can answer

1. **One sword or two?** Is the katana's blade the red light, or is the red light-sword a second weapon?
2. **Do his weapons only ever meet machines?** Robots power down; nothing alive is ever struck. That was assumed.
3. **When does he get them?** From the moment the suit forms in the backyard, or earned later?
4. **Does the katana replace Dragon Punch, or join it?**

The first three Tunnel Run questions are also still open: where it goes, who flies the dragon when Dad jumps, and whether switching is scripted or free.
